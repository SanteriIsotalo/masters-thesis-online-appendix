Springvest’s record-breaking year enables a
strong dividend payout. The highlights of 2024
include gaining thousands of new investor-clients
and facilitating our largest-ever funding rounds.
Our portfolio also saw several exits.
In 2024, Springvest Plc achieved growth in both revenue and
profitability. We have now facilitated over EUR 300 million in
funding for Finnish growth companies and are approaching 100
completed funding rounds. This year, we executed the largest
funding rounds in our history, collaborated with leading players
in Finland’s growth company ecosystem, and strengthened Finnish ownership in growth companies.
We also succeeded in acquiring new investor clients through
our funding rounds, welcoming around 2 000 new growth company investors. The profile of our growth investors has become
more professional: for example, in TILT Biotherapeutics’ EUR
12 million funding round, 37% of investments came from individual contributions exceeding EUR 100 000, and 51% from investments over EUR 50 000. Among our investors are founder
and family office entities, as well as highly professional private
investors.
Investors have long been anticipating the reopening of the exit
market, and last year we got a glimpse of this as five of Springvest’s portfolio companies exited. These and many other positive developments at Springvest also gained media attention, with
over 200 articles published about us and our business. The successes of 2024 provide an excellent foundation for Springvest
to continue delivering high-quality operations in 2025.
Our revenue, generated from investment services, amounted
to EUR 6.9 million during the reporting period (+83%). We facilitated a total of EUR 64 million in growth funding for Finnish growth companies (EUR 36.9 million, +73%). Despite investments
in growth, our operating profit reached EUR 2.0 million (EUR 0.5
million, +279%), with an operating profit margin of 30% (14%).
Earnings per share stood at EUR 0.29 (EUR 0.06, +387%).
Our equity ratio remained strong at 78%, and liquid assets
stood at EUR 3.5 million, despite distributing both regular dividends in May and an additional dividend in December. The
organisation, including tied agents, grew from 31 to 39 people,
while administrative staff increased from nine to eleven.
The number of Springvest shareholders also saw positive
growth, rising from 832 at the end of 2023 to 1,248 by the end
of 2024 (+50%). A growing shareholder base supports smooth
trading of Springvest shares.
The balance sheet value of our portfolio
companies increased by EUR 1 million
despite the exits.
Springvest is a Finland-based investment firm that connects
unlisted growth companies with investors. The company’s
income consists of cash and option-based fees on funding
rounds. Springvest focuses on selecting the most promising
growth companies, executing funding rounds, and supporting
the ownership and development of these companies. Returns
from these holdings may be realised in future exit events, which
is one of Springvest’s key value drivers.
Springvest received options worth approximately EUR 8
million as fees for funding rounds in 2024. About half of these
were registered within the year. The registered shares have
been included in the balance sheet value.
At the end of the year, the balance sheet value of Springvest’s portfolio companies was EUR 23.6 million, reflecting
a EUR 1.0 million increase during the reporting period (+5%).
These figures take into account the exited companies, as five of
our portfolio companies were sold or listed last year.
As of the end of the reporting period, Springvest has
ownership in a total of 37 growth companies.
The portfolio’s balance sheet value generated approximately
EUR1.7 million in net proceeds through portfolio company exits
during the financial year. This amount resulted from the exits of
Finbiosoft Oy, Convion Oy, Leadoo Marketing Technologies Oy,
and 9Solutions Oy, as well as Springvest’s sale of around 16%
of its holdings in Solar Foods Oyj following the company’s First
North listing.
The majority of last year’s exit proceeds, approximately
EUR1.2 million, were realised only after the interim financial
statements at the end of September. As a result, Springvest will
still have distributable funds from these exits for 2025. Springvest aims to be a reliable dividend payer, with a target of distributing at least 80% of both the financial year’s profit and the
proceeds from portfolio company exits as dividends.
We hold an average ownership stake of 3-6% in our 37 portfolio companies (as of 31 December 2024). We believe that
several of our portfolio companies have an excellent chance
of achieving an international breakthrough and a strong exit.
Over 60% (approximately EUR 15 million) of the balance
sheet value of Springvest’s portfolio companies is in the following growth companies: Desentum Oy, Solar Foods Oyj, TILT
Biotherapeutics Oy, Kuva Space Oy, Verge Motorcycles Oy, Sulapac Oy, Medixine Oy, and Paptic Oy. Sixteen companies in
our portfolio operate in the health and biotechnology sector,
ten in environmental technology, five in digital business, and six
in industrial or dual-use technologies.
We believe in the ability of Finnish
technology companies to grow, evolve, and
become market leaders in their fields.
Each year, Springvest selects around nine companies from
hundreds of applicants and builds a growth financing round
as a service. Over our ten-year history, we have consistently
identified companies with significant growth potential that also
attract Finnish investors. To date, we have provided Finnish
companies with over EUR 300 million in equity-based growth
funding. Nearly 100 financing rounds have been completed,
with a 100% success rate – each reaching at least its minimum
funding target.
During the reporting period, Springvest arranged financing
rounds for eight promising growth companies in the health and
technology sectors, as well as one company developing dualuse technology. The size of completed financing rounds grew
significantly compared to the previous year, averaging EUR 8.0
million, up from EUR 4.6 million in 2023. While Finland has over
40 early-stage venture capital investors, there are few growth
financing providers capable of delivering rounds of this scale
under a single service.
Six of the eight financing rounds we arranged were oversubscribed in an average of two weeks - about half the maximum duration of a round. The rounds reached up to 94% of
their maximum targets (2023: 69%), demonstrating an excellent
success rate.
Growth companies selected for Springvest’s financing
rounds are often at a pivotal stage, where years of development have started to pay off, the business has strong growth
potential, or an exit is on the horizon. Technology companies
aiming for rapid growth and international expansion typically
require multiple financing instruments throughout their lifecycle. In addition to equity and debt financing, growth companies should consider various grants and subsidies, particularly
for development and internationalisation projects. Scaling a
growth company to success is often a team effort - for example,
last year, we provided over EUR 20 million in financing to portfolio companies also backed by Lifeline, Voima, and Tesi. Bold entrepreneurship and skilled ownership
